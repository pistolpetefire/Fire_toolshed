Help · Flammable Vapor Spill Screener

This is a screening tool only. Enter every material property, correlation coefficient, and criterion with its source, edition, and section. Calculation is blocked if a required citation is missing.

  • Evaporation rate m may be entered directly or derived from a user-cited correlation (e.g. Mackay–Matsugu form) and spill area.
  • Vapor volume rate G = m·R·T/(P·MW).
  • Steady concentration C_ss = G / Q_eff where Q_eff = Q · K_mix.
  • Transient: C(t) = C_ss · (1 − e^(−Q_eff·t/V)).
  • Required exhaust Q = G / (K_mix · f · LFL).

If your source uses a safety factor K ≥ 1, enter K_mix = 1/K.
