Help & limits

Three distinct workflows (do not combine with engineered smoke control):

1. Prescriptive natural vents: Required area = floor area × user-verified ratio. Equipment count = ceil(required / vent size).

2. Prescriptive mechanical exhaust: Required cfm = volume × ACH / 60. Makeup-air area from stated velocity. Equipment count rounded up.

3. Idealized purge: Well-mixed exponential decay. C(t) = C0 exp(−ACH × t). Time to target and concentration curve.

Acceptance examples:
• 1:100 on 50,000 ft² → 500 ft²; 32 ft² vents → 16 units.
• 1,500,000 ft³ at 2 ACH → 50,000 cfm.
• 6 ACH, 10 min → ~36.8% remaining; 1% takes ~46.05 min.

Limits: No automatic exceptions, NFPA 92, tenability, or safe-reentry. Purge assumes clean air, good mixing, no continuing source unless entered.
