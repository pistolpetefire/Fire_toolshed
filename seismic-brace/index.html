Seismic Brace Load Calc

v1.0.2 · NFPA 13-2022 Ch. 18

User Guide

  • Wp = 1.15 × water-filled pipe weight in the zone of influence. Fpw = Cp × Wp.
  • Lateral braces at max 40 ft; longitudinal at max 80 ft.
  • Cp is an input (from project seismic data). Brace/fastener tables must be verified against a licensed copy of NFPA 13 before use.
