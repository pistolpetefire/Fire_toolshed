# Fire Tank Sizer changelog

- **1.4.0-prelim.** NFPA 1 required fire flow can be calculated on this page and applied to the tank: sprinklered buildings use 25% of the table flow, a 1,000 gpm floor (600 gpm with quick-response throughout), and a 2,000 gpm / 2 hour cap. The supply is the greater of fire flow and sprinkler demand, not the sum. Results are labeled AHJ may modify. 2021 cites §18.4.5.3 and §18.4.5.4; 2024 cites §18.4.4. When no prefab tank fits, a field-erected AWWA D100 screening list shows practical diameter, height, and calculated nominal gallons to confirm with the tank manufacturer. It is not a manufacturer's catalog. The Verification tab is loaded: `shared/verify-tab.js?v=1.4.0` and `assets/verify-cases.js?v=1.4.0` are in the page head, so the tab reports 8 of 8 instead of a blank status. Report logo options remain Benham, MeadHunt JV, AI&P + Benham (a Haskell Company), Haskell (by state), and upload. Help is unchanged.
- **1.1.1-prelim.** Report logo list gains a built-in option **AI&P + Benham (a Haskell Company)** (AI&P Alberti Ozzo & Partners + Benham). It is added once in the shared `shared/report-logo.js` (source key `aip`, image `shared/logos/aip-benham.png`), so it shows in this tool's Report logo list and preview and in the printout header. The default logo (Benham) and a saved choice (`fireToolshed.reportLogo.v1`) are unchanged. No layout changes.
