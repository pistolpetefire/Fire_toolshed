Simple Bulk SDS to HMIS Walkthrough

Clear step-by-step path from owner SDS package through AI classification batches, client quantities, MAQ review, and final HMIS room notes ready for Revit. Companion to the Simple instructions.

Duration ~10 minutes · Training examples only · No engineering conclusions issued

Full walkthrough Open bulk workspace Simple instructions Assessment / HMIS

What the walkthrough covers

  • Project and code basis setup (IBC, Florida Building Code, or NFPA 400)
  • Receiving the SDS package (PDFs or ZIP) and receipt log
  • Confirming documents and page ranges
  • Exporting controlled AI batches (25 recommended) and importing results
  • Downloading the prefilled owner workbook and applying returned quantities
  • Exporting the assessment and reviewing MAQ flags
  • Generating and exporting validated HMIS room notes text for Revit

Human controls every upload and validation. The app keeps the record. AI is used only for the classification batches you export and give to your AI yourself.

Using the training videos

Open the corresponding tool alongside the video. The Bulk SDS workspace stores one active workspace in your browser on this device — it is not shared team storage. Download a complete backup after important milestones.

The companion simple instructions linked from the tool provide the written sequence.
