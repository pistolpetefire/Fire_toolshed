Current boundaries

Standard rectilinear PDF viewport extraction is available. Proprietary scale formats, automatic drawing interpretation, direct provider connections and existing-calculator adapters remain unfinished. Measurements require coordinates and scale supplied by the AI’s document tools. No files are uploaded to or stored by this page.
