Standpipe Calculator

v1.0.9 · NFPA 14-2019

User Guide

  • Select class. Residual required at the top outlet is 100 psi (Class I/III) or 65 psi (Class II) per NFPA 14-2019 7.8.
  • Flow per NFPA 14-2019 7.10: 500 gpm first standpipe + 250 gpm each additional (capped), or 100 gpm for Class II.
  • Height is used for elevation (0.433 psi/ft). Friction is calculated with Hazen-Williams from pipe diameter, length, and C-factor (120 wet steel / 100 dry typical).
  • Required pressure at base = residual at top + elevation + friction loss.
