Pressure Tank (Boyle’s Law)

v1.0.3 · NFPA 22 pressure tanks

User Guide

  • Enter total tank volume, pre-charge (or initial air) pressure, high and low operating pressures, and atmospheric pressure.
  • The tool uses Boyle’s law with absolute pressures to find the air volume at high and low pressure and the usable water volume (drawdown).
  • Intended as a tab on Fire Tank Sizer; existing volume results stay unchanged.
