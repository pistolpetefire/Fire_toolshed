Fire Flow Calculator — Help

Purpose: Preliminary required fire flow at 20 psi residual, plus duration, for a new building. Not a sealed design. AHJ may modify.

Code basis and edition

  • NFPA 1 / Florida FFPC: Sprinklered throughout: 25% of the table flow, not less than 1,000 gpm, not more than 2,000 gpm, duration not more than 2 hours. Quick-response sprinklers throughout lower the minimum to 600 gpm.
  • Edition: 2021 labels the rules NFPA 1 §18.4.5.3 (reduction, floors, cap) and §18.4.5.4 (greater of fire flow and sprinkler demand). 2024 labels the same rules §18.4.4. The math does not change. This tool does not invent 2024 subsection numbers.
  • IBC / IFC Appendix B: Keeps this calculator's IFC reduction (25% with a 1,000 gpm floor, 1,500 gpm for NFPA 13R, 50% and 500 gpm for NFPA 13D). The NFPA 1 2,000 gpm / 2 hour cap is not applied on the IFC basis. Duration follows the reduced flow.
  • UFC 3-600-01: A standard sprinklered building uses the NFPA 1 reduction. The listed family-housing, vehicle, and aircraft values are fixed and are not reduced again. DFPE may modify.

Inputs

  • Construction type: NFPA 1 types grouped the same way as the IBC types in the toolshed area bands. Type II (000) is in the IIB group. Type V (000) is VB.
  • Area: Fire-flow calculation area in square feet. The lookup is the toolshed copy of the area bands, not a new transcription from the code book.
  • Table flow and table duration overrides: Type the adopted table's flow and duration when you do not want the lookup. Leave both blank to use the lookup. Do not type a guessed table value.
  • Sprinkler system and quick-response: "Sprinklered throughout" turns on the NFPA 1 reduction. Quick-response throughout changes only the NFPA 1 floor, from 1,000 gpm to 600 gpm. It does not change the IFC path.
  • Sprinkler demand and duration: Optional. The required supply rate is the greater of fire-flow gpm and sprinkler-demand gpm. The storage volume is the greater of the two volumes. The two demands are not added.
  • Report logo: Benham, MeadHunt JV, AI&P + Benham (a Haskell Company), Haskell by state, or an uploaded image. The choice is shared with the other Toolshed report tools.

Result

  • Fire flow is reported at 20 psi residual. Gallons are flow × duration × 60, before the tank sizer's safety and unusable allowances.
  • A sprinklered NFPA 1 result is labeled AHJ may modify.
  • The hydrant count is this app's older planning estimate. It is not IFC Table C102.1 and not an NFPA 1 hydrant-credit table.
  • Capture for Pump & Tank stores the supply rate and the governing volume for the Fire Tank Sizer.
