Help & limits

Purpose: Estimate steel temperature histories under specified fire exposure. Not capacity or rating.

Unprotected lumped capacitance:
dT/dt = (F/A) / (ρ c) × [h (Tg − T) + ε σ (Tg⁴ − T⁴)]

Convection-only analytical (constant Tg):
T(t) = Tg − (Tg − T0) exp(−[h (F/A)/(ρ c)] t)

Protected model: Separate validated formulation using protection thickness and thermal properties (user-verified).

Timestep: Halving dt should converge within documented tolerance.

Limits: State lumped-temperature and partial-exposure applicability. No structural stability, connections, restraint, spalling, or fire-rating. Timber char only as separate verified module.
