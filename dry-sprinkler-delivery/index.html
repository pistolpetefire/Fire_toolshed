Methodology & assumptions

  • Eligible systems only: dry pipe and double-interlock preaction. Wet pipe is rejected for water delivery compliance (water is already at the heads). Single-interlock is not evaluated here.
  • Tree / simple branched networks only (no full grids in v1).
  • Volume: π/4 · (ID/12)² · L · 7.4805 gal; optional fitting volume ≈ 15% of EL as pipe.
  • Hazen–Williams: p = 4.52 Q1.85 / (C1.85 d4.87) psi/ft; elev 0.433 psi/ft.
  • Trip: t ≈ 0.0352 · (VT/(An√T₀)) · ln(pa0/pa); QOD applies 50% planning reduction.
  • Transit: t = 60 · Vremote / Qfill; Qfill from residual @ DPV via inverse HW, or override.
  • Bands: Green ≤90% of limit; Yellow within 10% (still ≤ limit); Red exceeds.
  • Does not model compressible multi-phase flow, accelerator dynamics, or listed software methods.
  • Not a substitute for listed calculation programs or field trip testing.
