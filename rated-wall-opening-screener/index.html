Help & limits

Purpose: Separately screen opening geometry, protective reliability, and radiant exposure.

Geometry: Aggregate opening width / wall length compared to user-entered limit. Equality meets the limit.

Reliability: Transparent AND/OR for independent probabilities. OR = 1 − (1−p1)(1−p2). AND = p1×p2. Common-cause added explicitly. Per demand ≠ rate ≠ unavailability.

Radiation: Rectangular source view-factor approximation. Net flux ∝ εσ(T⁴−T_amb⁴)×F(distance). Zero ΔT → zero net flux. Distance reduces flux.

Do not invent failure probabilities, temperatures, emissivity, or criteria. Identify all sources.

Limits: No equivalency approval or fire-rating prediction. Protective rating does not establish unexposed-face temperature.
