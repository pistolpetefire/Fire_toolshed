Velocity / Suction Check

v1.0.3 · NFPA 20 suction velocity

User Guide

  • Enter the pump rated flow and the internal diameter of the suction pipe.
  • The tool calculates velocity at 150% of rated flow using v = 0.4085 × Q / d².
  • NFPA 20 limits suction velocity to 15 ft/s at 150% flow to reduce turbulence and cavitation risk.
  • Intended to be added to Fire Pump Sizer.
