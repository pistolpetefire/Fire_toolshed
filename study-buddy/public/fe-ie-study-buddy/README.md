# FE Industrial Practice

Intern trainer for **FE Industrial and Systems** — the old paper **afternoon industrial** topics (economy, quality, work design, production, facilities, human factors, management, modeling, reliability). Not an official NCEES exam.

Morning / general math lives in the sibling app **FE General Math Practice**.

**Diagnostic first (30 Q, 3 per industrial topic), then drill weak topics from 1,100+ MCQs.** Retake form B to confirm a topic moved to strong.
