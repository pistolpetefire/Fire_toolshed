User manual

  1. Take the diagnostic (27 questions, 3 per topic).
  2. Read the weak / ok / strong map.
  3. Drill weak topics from the 1,100+ bank. Workshop on paper.
  4. Retake diagnostic B to confirm a topic moved to strong.
  5. Clear Review missed. Do one timed mixed set at FE pace (~2.5 min each).
  6. During diagnostics and timed sets, open Handbook and search with Ctrl+F (or ⌘F) — same condition as the FE CBT.
