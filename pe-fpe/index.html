User manual

Practice tool for the April 13, 2027 PE Fire Protection CBT. Not an NCEES product.

Modes

  • Exam — NDA, tutorial, working clock, one optional 50:00 break, review, irreversible submit. Solutions after submit only.
  • Coach — same items; check after each item; pacing toast if you average more than 7 min/item.
  • Drill — untimed, filter by domain, immediate scoring.
  • Keep sharp — reinforcement for topics you already hit. Same items, shuffled, coach feedback. Getting one wrong drops it onto the repair list.
  • Repair — only the items you missed. Do not skip Keep sharp just because a domain looks green.

During an item

  • Every item is four-option multiple choice (A–D). Click to select. Right-click / long-press to strike out without selecting.
  • Flag marks the item yellow on the navigator and review screen.
  • Split view: item left; Locus index, My PDFs, and Calculator on the right.
  • Break pauses the working clock once (practice default is a clean pause).

References

  • The locus index is handbook chapter titles plus listed-code section titles. It is not the NCEES handbook body.
  • Download the official handbook from MyNCEES, then Upload PDF on the home page. The file stays in this browser only.
  • Open the My PDFs tab and Ctrl+F / ⌘F — same habit as Pearson.
  • SFPE Handbook of Fire Protection Engineering (5th or 6th Ed.) is further reading after a miss. It is not in the exam room and is not a scoring key. Solutions list the chapter to read, then send you back to the NCEES handbook locus or listed NFPA.
  • Do not confuse three books: NCEES PE Fire Protection Reference Handbook (exam day), SFPE Handbook of Fire Protection Engineering (study), NFPA Fire Protection Handbook (also not supplied on exam day).

Scoring

  • 1 or 0 per item. No partial credit.
  • After submit: raw 0–n, domain heat, miss types, items over 9 minutes, repair list of at most 15 loci.
  • This tool does not invent an official cut score.

Reset

Home → Reset this browser clears the saved session and results. You can also drop uploaded PDFs. Your files on disk are not deleted.
