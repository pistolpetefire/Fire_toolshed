# Handbook TOC + listed-code section index (titles only)

This index is a **search practice surface**. It is not a reprint of the NCEES PE Fire Protection Reference Handbook and not a reprint of any NFPA standard.

- Handbook entries: section numbers + titles from the publicly listed contents pages of *PE Fire Protection Reference Handbook* Version 1.2 (© NCEES). No equations, tables, or figures.
- Code entries: edition-locked section **numbers and titles** commonly cited on the April 2027 spec sheet. No standard body text.
- Download the handbook from [MyNCEES](https://account.ncees.org/reference-handbooks/). Attach your own PDF on exam day; this pane trains *where to look*.
