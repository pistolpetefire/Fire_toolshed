Where is hose flow applied?

Upstream hose flow affects a flow-test supply curve but does not pass through this lead-in. Lead-in hose flow branches off before the BFP. Include any inside hose flowing through the riser in the riser demand. Fixed pressure must already represent the total simultaneous supply flow.

03 Lead-in & riser
