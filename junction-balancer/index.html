Fire Toolshed

Junction Balancer / Equivalent-K

v1.0.2 · NFPA 13 hydraulic junction points

User Guide

  • Enter the calculated flow and pressure at the first junction (Q1, P1).
  • Enter the actual pressure available at the second junction (P2).
  • The tool computes the adjusted flow Q2 = Q1 × √(P2/P1) and the equivalent K of the line (Keq = Q1 / √P1).
  • It checks whether the pressure difference is within the normal 0.5 psi balance tolerance used at hydraulic junction points.
  • Carry the highest pressure and the adjusted flows forward in the calculation.
