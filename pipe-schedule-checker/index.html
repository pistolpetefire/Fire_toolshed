Fire Toolshed

Pipe Schedule Checker

v1.0.0 · NFPA 13 light & ordinary hazard steel

User Guide

  • Select hazard (Light or Ordinary) and pipe size.
  • Enter the number of sprinklers supplied by that pipe section and all downstream piping.
  • The tool shows the maximum allowed by the NFPA 13 pipe schedule tables and whether your count is within limits.
  • Pipe schedule is permitted only for Light and Ordinary Hazard. Exceeding the limit requires a full hydraulic calculation.
