Pump + Supply Curve Overlay

v1.0.1 · NFPA 20 pump + city supply

User Guide

  • Enter city static pressure, residual pressure and the flow at which the residual was measured.
  • Enter pump rated flow and rated net pressure.
  • Enter the system demand flow and pressure.
  • The tool approximates the city curve (N^1.85 style) and a simple pump net curve, adds them, and reports the margin at demand and the pressure at 150% flow.
  • Intended to extend the existing chart in Fire Pump Sizer. Confirm with the manufacturer pump curve for final design.
